## Remaining interpretation warnings, not release defects

1. **External transfer is descriptive.** Prior-unexposed external records have 618,537/1,092,838 valid windows (56.6%), whereas train has 230,452/232,332 (99.2%). Event rates use scored-center exposure, but differences in dataset, channels, referencing and artifact mix can still dominate the rate shift. [Experiment 007](../notes/experiment-007.md#L17) says this and keeps exposed records separate. Do not turn timing agreement or rate shift into brain-state accuracy, a diagnosis or a stable token vocabulary.
2. **Phase controls have thin agreement support.** In the 16 external prior-unexposed control records, independent phase has 2,174 valid windows versus original's 3,265. Its shape/spectrum F1 median of zero draws from only **two** records; its spectrum/coordination aggregate is undefined. Shared phase also has varying, small F1 denominators. The table displays these counts and [Experiment 006](../notes/experiment-006.md#L19) describes the tests as bounded, one-draw perturbations. They do not establish biological specificity or statistical significance.
3. **Geometry is not universal or semantic.** The five values describe trajectories in three different feature coordinate systems; an endpoint return distance is not recovery time. [Experiment 003](../notes/experiment-003.md#L41) states the limitation. The website also states that the representations are LLM-authored numerical choices, not independent pretrained LLMs discovering language.
