## Question and measurement levels

Do independently measured temporal landmarks recur at matching times and have reproducible geometry across sessions, sources and representations? Agreement is a measurement result; it does not establish a universal token alphabet or a diagnostic state.

| Level | Operational object | Time and units | Interpretation boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waveform extrema | Sign change of a smoothed first derivative, with peak/trough polarity retained | Native sample index and seconds; amplitude in microvolts | A voltage turning point, not a state transition |
| Waveform inflection | Sign change of a smoothed second derivative with nonzero first derivative | Seconds; slope in microvolts/second; curvature in microvolts/second squared | A scale-dependent bend in the observed waveform |
| Cycle | Consecutive same-polarity extrema and the intervening opposite extremum | Period in seconds, amplitude in microvolts, rise and peak fractions dimensionless | A candidate oscillatory cycle; irregular/noisy cycles remain flagged |
| Burst | Contiguous accepted cycles or a separately defined envelope interval | Half-open start/end interval, seconds and cycle count | A detector-defined oscillation episode |
| Feature change | Existing multivariate before/after difference at fixed scales | Seconds and standardized score | Slower feature change; current implementation is not a cycle detector |
| State | A later fitted grouping with fixed boundaries | Not implemented here | No biological state names or diagnoses enter discovery |

The cycle branch builds on [Cole and Voytek's method](https://doi.org/10.1152/jn.00273.2019): cycle duration, amplitude and symmetry can be measured separately, and oscillatory validity must be assessed before interpreting those measurements. The [author implementation](https://bycycle-tools.github.io/bycycle/auto_tutorials/plot_2_bycycle_algorithm.html) uses a narrowband signal to locate candidate cycles and a less restricted signal to measure their shape. Its settings depend on noise and filtering. These are reasons to test parameter sensitivity, not evidence that tutorial defaults generalize to around-ear EEG.

## Shared input and validity contract

Discovery receives only numeric `samples_uv[channel, sample]`, positive `sample_rate_hz`, a same-shape Boolean validity mask, and numeric per-channel timing segments. Curator keys, source identities, device/reference descriptions and hashes stay in a separate provenance table and join only in evaluation. Discovery still needs calibration, timing and validity; suppressing those would discard necessary measurement information.

Events use the native sampling clock. No upsampling supplies new precision. Version 1 requires at least 200 Hz for waveform derivatives; lower-rate records return `UNSUPPORTED_RATE` for that branch while remaining eligible for compatible slower methods. Do not silently resample or reinterpret units. Each uninterrupted valid run is processed separately. No filtering, interpolation, cycle, burst, derivative or event match may bridge a gap, a rejected sample, a clock reset, a channel/reference change or a source-file boundary. Preserve rejected durations and reasons.

Intervals are `[start_sample, end_sample)` and relative to an explicit continuous segment. Instantaneous events retain an integer index and may retain a deterministic fractional interpolation separately. The native sample period is a resolution floor, not a confidence interval. Record filter/smoothing support, gap distance, disagreement across settings and synthetic localization errors as separate uncertainty fields.

## Fixed candidate settings for synthetic verification

These are transparent engineering choices for testing, not patterns discovered in brains. No setting is chosen by maximizing model agreement or by looking at reserved people.

1. Primary waveform branch: fourth-order Butterworth 1–40 Hz, second-order-section zero-phase implementation, applied independently within valid runs. Keep the unfiltered calibrated trace for inspection. Reuse the repository's gap-safe conventions, but do not silently reuse its windowwise outputs as a continuous trace. No notch is primary because 50/60 Hz is outside this passband; explicitly assess leakage and ringing.
2. On the declared 1–40 Hz float64 filtered trace, calculate derivatives with `scipy.signal.savgol_filter(polyorder=3, deriv=1 or 2, delta=1/sample_rate_hz, mode='interp')`. The target window is 31 ms; choose the nearest odd integer to `0.031 * sample_rate_hz`, at least five, with ties upward. Record both `window_samples / fs` and its actual half-support `(window_samples-1)/(2*fs)`. Sensitivities target 21 and 51 ms. For derivative order k, values with magnitude at most `64 * float64_epsilon * max(1 U_k, median(abs(d_k)))` are numerical zero, where U1 is 1 microvolt/second and U2 is 1 microvolt/second squared. All medians use that channel's guarded valid run. These are numerical conventions, not physiological thresholds.
3. Emit a crossing for adjacent opposite-sign derivative samples or one maximal zero run bracketed by opposite signs. A retained event needs two consecutive qualified nonzero samples of each sign on its respective side, all within valid support. Direct crossings use the first sample to the right; zero runs use the midpoint rounded down. Optional fractional indices use linear interpolation on the bracketing derivative values for a direct crossing only; zero-run fractional indices are null. First-derivative positive-to-negative crossings are peaks and the reverse troughs. Inflections use second-derivative crossings and the first derivative at the integer event index, requiring absolute slope above `1e-6 * max(1 microvolt/second, median(abs(d1)))`. Preserve candidates and rejection reasons. Before filtering, a native run with fewer than three samples or `max(abs(diff(x,n=2))) <= 64*float64_epsilon*max(1 microvolt,max(abs(x)))` returns DEGENERATE_AFFINE and no accepted waveform events. Constant and affine abstention is numerical, not physiological.
4. Pin NumPy1.26.4, SciPy1.14.1, bycycle1.2.0 and NeuroDSP2.3.0. For the primary SOS chain use `padtype='odd'` and explicit `padlen=3*(2*n_sections+1-min(count(sos[:,2]==0),count(sos[:,5]==0)))`, matching the pinned SciPy convention. Each optional notch uses odd padding with `padlen=3*max(len(a),len(b))`. Before real data, measure the complete chain's guard at every rate using a 60-second centered unit impulse and centered unit step. For both tails, find the support beyond which remaining impulse energy is below1e-6 of total and absolute step response stays below1e-6 of its peak. Take the maximum of these two-sided bounds and half the smoothing support. The input to a cycle/burst chain includes its preceding broadband filter; its support must include both filters. An unresolved60-second support probe is UNSUPPORTED_FILTER_SUPPORT. A run too short for padding or with no guarded interior is UNSUPPORTED_SHORT_RUN. Entire event support, both cycle endpoints and all intervening points must lie inside one guarded valid run. Burst boundaries touching guards are censored and excluded from boundary matching. Record guard and rejected duration. Offline zero-phase timing is not real-time latency.
5. Cycles: use pinned bycycle1.2.0 on the 1–40 Hz trace separately for each channel, continuous run and band4–8,8–13,13–30 Hz. Declare `center_extrema='peak'` (trough-to-trough), `burst_method='cycles'`, `return_samples=True`, thresholds `amp_fraction_threshold=.3`, `amp_consistency_threshold=.4`, `period_consistency_threshold=.5`, `monotonicity_threshold=.8`, `min_n_cycles=3`. Set `find_extrema_kwargs` to `pad=False, boundary=0, filter_kwargs={'n_cycles':3}`. Do not supply the `first_extrema` key: pinned `compute_shape_features` rejects that override, even when its value is `peak`; its internal peak-first convention is fixed. Explicit `center_extrema='peak'` retains trough-to-trough cycles; retain/hash actual filter coefficients and effective support from the pinned library. No threshold reduction or edge recomputation. Preserve every cycle and is_burst flag, including unscorable edge cycles. Map library sample columns back to segment-relative indices; period_seconds=period/fs and amplitude_uv=volt_amp. Intervals are [sample_last_trough,sample_next_trough). These literature-guided bands/settings are measured hypotheses, not natural token classes or evidence of validated ear-EEG performance.
6. Bursts: the cycle definition is a maximal sequence of adjacent valid is_burst cycles whose half-open intervals touch. A separate NeuroDSP2.3.0 dual-threshold definition runs on each same broadband-filtered continuous channel run with f_range=band, dual_thresh=(1,2), avg_type='median', magnitude_type='amplitude', min_n_cycles=3 and n_cycles=3 for its filter. Retain/hash actual filter coefficients and full chain support. Normalize the amplitude over that run; keep median value and interval in the receipt. True-mask runs become half-open intervals; the duration floor is3/band_lower_hz seconds, not three detected cycle rows. Censor guard-touching boundaries. A constant-amplitude oscillator can have no envelope bursts. Keep the two definitions separate; never select whichever yields stronger agreement.

Waveform sensitivities include 0.5–40 and 1–30 Hz passbands, polarity inversion, scale changes, constant offsets, reference changes supported by real contacts, and known 50/60 Hz contamination. A 50 Hz notch, a 60 Hz notch and no notch are named comparisons with separate filter support. Historical CodeBrain preprocessing remains unchanged; new comparisons receive their own protocol. Nonlinear artifact injection and gain/clipping changes must be distinguishable from physiology.

## Event records and matching

One table row per candidate event includes: schema/protocol version, opaque segment/channel key, family, detector, parameter hash, integer index, optional fractional index, seconds from segment start, interval end if applicable, polarity/direction, amplitude, slope, curvature, period, rise/peak fractions, cycle/burst membership, accepted flag, rejection reasons, support interval and uncertainty fields. Fields inapplicable to an event are null, never zero-filled. Curator tables hold recording identifiers, source/mask/code hashes, original rate/units, clock mapping and reference.

Compare only event lists on a verified shared clock, per aligned segment/channel and intersection of their guarded valid timelines. Include an event only when its entire support lies inside the intersection. If the clock-error bound is unknown or not below tolerance, return NOT_COMPARABLE. Reuse the existing matcher's maximum-cardinality/minimum-total-absolute-error objective in a new event wrapper without altering historical outputs. Expose deterministic matched input-index pairs and compute signed lag as t_B minus t_A. Preserve the helper's tie ordering and prove it with fixtures. Eligible-pair overflow returns MATCH_LIMIT rather than pruning. Before/after counts and exact common duration are mandatory. Empty/empty agreement and lags are null, not1; with one empty list, fractions are zero only where the denominator is nonzero.

- Waveform landmark comparisons: primary tolerance 25 ms; sensitivities 10 and 50 ms. Report both directional matched fractions, `2 * matches / (n_A + n_B)`, median signed lag and median absolute lag. Empty/empty agreement is undefined, not 100%.
- Burst boundaries: primary tolerance 100 ms, plus interval intersection-over-union and duration bias. Match only within the same band/definition unless a cross-definition comparison is explicitly labeled.
- Feature/model boundaries: retain the existing 1-second tolerance initially; do not claim 25-ms agreement from embeddings with one-second or coarser temporal support. Record patch support and timestamp convention. Different methods may require aggregation into the common coarser timeline; that becomes a distinct estimand.

For v1 waveform events, geometry consists of filtered voltage at the landmark (microvolts), d1 (microvolts/second), d2 (microvolts/second squared), and preceding/following intervals to the same family and polarity/direction, provided both neighbors have valid support in the same run. Add the pinned cycle amplitude, period and symmetry fractions. Path length, turning angle and return distance remain null for waveform events. Existing transition_geometry applies only to feature/model boundaries on its own standardized multivariate grid; a cross-family mapping requires a later frozen definition. Token IDs from independent models are not compared literally.

## Controls, unit of evidence and stopping rules

Synthetic battery: analytic sine and chirp extrema/inflections; independently varied amplitude, frequency and asymmetric cycles; finite oscillation packets with known boundaries; flat/linear traces; white and colored noise; isolated spikes and steps; 50/60 Hz contamination; saturation, polarity/gain changes, gaps and clock resets. Include noise-level sweeps and at least 100 fixed seeds for stochastic controls. Freeze generated inputs and analytic/event truth before fitting detector settings. Avoid presenting filtered noise as proof of biological oscillation.

Acceptance for the implementation: correct units and deterministic results; <=2-native-sample localization error for frozen stationary analytic sinusoids at in-band frequencies3,5,10,20Hz, phases0,pi/7,pi/2 and rates200,250,500Hz, evaluated away from guarded support; exact constant/affine abstention; no accepted support crossing gaps; null missing values; one-to-one matching invariants and correct burst units. Chirps, asymmetric cycles and finite packets instead report signed bias, missed/extra landmarks and boundary error by configuration; filtering can move their native landmarks. Report derivative landmark rate per valid minute on white/colored noise and artifacts; only cycle/burst detections on a generator explicitly lacking an oscillator are labeled false detections. Report packet hit rates and false bursts across noise levels with frozen negative-trial units. Zero-valid-duration rates are undefined. No arbitrary specificity gate is added; a failed detector is excluded from confirmatory use and its failure is published.

Real-data controls retain within-channel phase-randomized and shared-phase surrogates as different nulls, time shifts that preserve within-run continuity, and reference/artifact sensitivity. Phase surrogates do not preserve waveform morphology and are not a universal null. Freeze the realizations and source exposure ledger. Treat people as the primary independent sampling unit; session, file, window, event and source-hour counts are separate denominators. Use participant/recording-aware resampling, not millions of event rows as independent people. Predeclare primary metrics and correction families in the numbered experiment, and publish all specified sensitivities.
