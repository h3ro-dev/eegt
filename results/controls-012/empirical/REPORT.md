At 10 and 50 ms the per-block raw-minus-null ranges are 0.0780–0.1209 and 0.1063–0.1174, respectively; full row results and null order statistics are in the JSON stage receipts.

The null fixes per-channel/event-stratum counts, 1-second-bin occupancy, accepted guarded interval and each phase event's support width while randomizing feasible fine timing on the phase side. It breaks fine spacing, refractory structure, cross-channel synchrony and realizable waveform constraints. The observed contrast therefore does **not** identify a neural mechanism or distinguish biology from shared artifact, reference, gain, filtering, clock, detector or audio coupling. No participant-level p value or confidence interval was computed. The one-block-per-session selection and six-person denominator are too limited for population inference or reliability assessment; untouched-person replication needs its own pre-access freeze and qualification audit.
